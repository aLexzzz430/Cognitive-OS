**Claude‑Style AGI Blueprint (≤ 100 words)**  

1. **Neuro‑Symbolic Core** – a unified model that merges dense transformer embeddings (for perception & language) with a graph‑based symbolic layer (for reasoning, planning, and explicit knowledge).  

2. **Meta‑Learning Scheduler** – a reinforcement‑driven controller that dynamically selects and re‑configures sub‑modules (e.g., vision, memory, tool use) based on task context, enabling rapid adaptation without catastrophic forgetting.  

3. **Reflective Memory Bank** – a long‑term, episodic‑semantic hybrid store that indexes past interactions, abstracts patterns, and provides “self‑questioning” prompts for continual self‑improvement.  

**Unique Feature – Intent‑Alignment Loop** – an inner‑feedback circuit that continuously projects the model’s tentative outputs onto a value‑grounded utility vector, allowing real‑time safety alignment and goal consistency.
