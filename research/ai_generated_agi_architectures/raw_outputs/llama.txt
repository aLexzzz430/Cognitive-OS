**Testable Architecture for a General-Purpose Cognitive System**

**Memory**: A hierarchical, graph-based memory stores observations, actions, and outcomes. Each node represents a state, and edges connect nodes with similar observations.

**Representation of the World**: A 3D representation of the warehouse, including rooms, crates, and obstacles. The representation is updated based on observations and actions.

**Uncertainty**: A probabilistic graph network models uncertainty about the world, incorporating observations, actions, and outcomes.

**Planning**: A planning algorithm generates sequences of actions to achieve goals, considering uncertainty and potential failures.

**Learning from Corrections**: The system learns from corrections by updating the representation of the world and uncertainty.

**Tool/Action Execution**: A robotic arm executes actions, with safety features to prevent damage.

**Safety and Human Approval**: Human approval is required for critical actions, and safety features prevent accidents.

**Persistence/Replay**: The system persists and replays experiences to improve planning and learning.

**Coordination**: One agent suffices, as the system can coordinate actions through planning and learning.

**Evaluation**: A falsifiable evaluation measures the system's ability to plan and execute delivery tasks with a high success rate (90%) and a low failure rate (10%) within 10 attempts. Failure criterion: the system fails to deliver a crate within 5 attempts.

**Smallest Buildable First Step**: Implementing a probabilistic graph network to model uncertainty.

**Non-Obvious Trade-off**: Balancing exploration and exploitation in planning and learning, as excessive exploration can lead to slow progress, while excessive exploitation can result in suboptimal solutions.
