# Mistral — research reproduction

## Memory architecture
Compact working memory, provenance-aware knowledge store, and skill registry filtered by scope and freshness.

## Reasoning and planning loop
Compile goals into typed operation graphs, route specialists, verify results, repair, and preserve replay traces.

## Learning and self-improvement
Offline traces and evaluator labels improve routing, skills, and adapters; canary and regression gates required.

## Tools and action
Structured tool graph validates permissions, supports dry runs/retries, and marks irreversible operations for approval.

## World model and safety
Compositional state representation with domain predictors; least privilege, isolation, monitoring, rate limits, and revocation.
