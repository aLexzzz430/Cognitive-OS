# grok-4.5

A complete AGI architecture could use a **modular cognitive core** with three layers:

- **Memory:**  
  - **Working memory** for current goals and context.  
  - **Episodic memory** for time-stamped experiences and outcomes.  
  - **Semantic memory** for compressed world knowledge.  
  - **Procedural memory** for learned skills and policies.  
  - All memories are linked by a retrieval system with relevance, confidence, and recency scoring.

- **Reasoning:**  
  - A **planner** decomposes goals into subgoals.
