# xAI Grok — research reproduction

## Memory architecture
Fast recent cache, searchable event history, and semantic index; live information expires unless verified.

## Reasoning and planning loop
Gather context, form and challenge hypotheses, then use observe-act with rapid replanning and coordinated parallel subplans.

## Learning and self-improvement
Offline updates from traces and rewards; narrow online routing/retrieval changes only until safety is shown.

## Tools and action
Typed affordances with timeouts, budgets, confirmation for irreversible effects, and trace logging.

## World model and safety
Task-local predictive scratch model and confidence labels; access control, monitoring, sandboxing, and kill switches.
