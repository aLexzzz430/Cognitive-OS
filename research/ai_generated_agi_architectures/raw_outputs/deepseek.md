**High‑Level AGI Architecture**

1. **Cognitive Core** – a transformer‑based reasoning engine that integrates symbolic manipulation, neural inference, and few‑shot learning to generate plans and explanations.  
2. **World Model** – a multimodal generative simulator that continuously updates a latent representation of physical, social, and linguistic dynamics from sensor streams.  
3. **Adaptive Memory** – a hierarchical, differentiable memory system that stores episodic experiences, abstracts concepts, and provides rapid retrieval for context‑aware decision making.  

**Unique Feature:** *Self‑Reflective Metacognitive Loop* – the system monitors its own confidence, detects reasoning gaps, and autonomously triggers targeted rehearsal or model‑updates, enabling continual self‑improvement without external supervision.
