## 6. Safety
Formal verification of all system behaviors. Theorem-prover-based guardrails. Mathematically provable safety properties.

## 7. Evaluation
Formal theorem proving for correctness. Empirical testing for performance. Verified capability claims.

## 8. Persistence
Persistent knowledge graph with formal consistency guarantees. Verified state transitions.

Symbolic planner orchestrates neural specialists. Formal coordination protocols. Verified agent interactions.
